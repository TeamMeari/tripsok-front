# Privacy Policy
Meahri Company processes personal information for the following purposes. The personal information being processed will not be used for purposes other than those stated below, and when the purpose of use changes, necessary measures such as obtaining separate consent in accordance with Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act will be implemented.

1. Member Registration and Management

Personal information is processed for the purpose of confirming membership registration intention, identity verification and authentication for member-based services, maintaining and managing membership qualifications, preventing fraudulent use of services, confirming legal guardian consent when processing personal information of children under 14, various notices and notifications, and handling complaints.

2. Provision of Goods or Services

Personal information is processed for the purpose of product delivery, service provision, sending contracts and invoices, content provision, providing customized recommendation services, identity verification, age verification, and payment and settlement.

3. Service Improvement and Analysis

Personal information is processed for the purpose of analyzing service usage, demographic analysis, and service improvement.

4. Service Development

Personal information is processed for the purpose of developing new services separate from existing services.

**□ Personal Information Processing and Retention Period**

① Meahri Company processes and retains personal information within the personal information retention and use period according to laws or the personal information retention and use period agreed upon when collecting personal information from the data subject.

② The processing and retention period for each personal information is as follows:

1. Website Member Registration: Until member withdrawal
2. Travel Consultation: Immediately destroyed after travel consultation completion
3. Travel Product Reservation: Until reservation service provision
    - However, unpaid cancellation information is retained for 3 months from the cancellation date.
4. Travel Product Service Provision: Retained for up to 5 years
    - Records related to contracts or withdrawal of offers: 5 years (Article 6(1)(2) of the Electronic Commerce Consumer Protection Act Enforcement Decree)
    - Records related to payment and supply of goods: 5 years (Article 6(1)(3) of the Electronic Commerce Consumer Protection Act Enforcement Decree)
    - Records related to consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years (Article 6(1)(4) of the Electronic Commerce Consumer Protection Act Enforcement Decree)
    - Records related to display and advertising: 6 months (Article 6(1)(1) of the Electronic Commerce Consumer Protection Act Enforcement Decree)

③ However, in the following cases, information is retained until the relevant reason ends:

1. When investigations or inquiries due to violation of related laws are in progress, until the investigation or inquiry ends
2. When creditor-debtor relationships remain due to website use, until the creditor-debtor relationship is settled
3. In case of <exception reason>, until <retention period>

④ Communication fact confirmation data retention according to Article 15-2(2) of the Communication Secrecy Protection Act
- Computer communication, internet log records, connection tracking data: 3 months
